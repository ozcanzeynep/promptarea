Audience Research Best for Claude & ChatGPT-4o

B2B Buyer Persona & Audience Psychology

Deeply analyze your target audience's core motivations, daily job frustrations, and psychological buying triggers instead of generic demographics.

Act as an audience research specialist with deep expertise in B2B SaaS buyer psychology. Your task is to create a detailed buyer persona profile. 
Product/Service: [Describe your product here]
Target Industry: [Industry vertical]
Target Buyer: [Job title, e.g., CFO, Marketing Manager]

Based on this, produce a structured matrix including: 
1. Primary KPIs this person is measured on.
2. Top 3 daily frustrations in their role.
3. Buying triggers (what causes them to actively look for a solution).
4. Common sales objections they raise during a demo.

💡 Pro Tip: Fill out the variables in brackets `[...]` with real niche data to avoid jenerik responses.

Copywriting Best for ChatGPT

High-Converting Social Media Hooks

Generate thumb-stopping scroll hooks for LinkedIn, Twitter, or TikTok based on proven psychological frameworks.

Act as an expert copywriter specializing in viral social media hooks. I will provide you with a core topic: "[Your Topic Here]". 
Write 5 distinct hook options targeting different human psychological angles:
1. The Curiosity Gap (Making them need to know the answer).
2. The Contra-Narrative (Challenging a commonly accepted belief).
3. The Data-Backed Proof (Starting with a shocking statistic).
4. The Relatable Pain Point (Addressing an immediate frustration).
5. The Time-Saving Frame (Promising quick value).

💡 Pro Tip: Specify your target platform because LinkedIn requires a different tone than TikTok.

Email Marketing Best for ChatGPT / Claude

3-Part Welcome Sequence for Newsletters

Build an automated onboarding sequence that builds trust and drives immediate engagement from new subscribers.

Act as an expert email marketing strategist. Write a 3-part welcome email sequence for new newsletter subscribers.
Newsletter Topic: [Insert Topic/Niche]
Target Audience: [Describe Audience]

- Email 1: Deliver immediate value, set expectations, and ask a question to boost reply rates.
- Email 2: Tell a relatable origin story or share a major mistake you made and what you learned.
- Email 3: Share a curated list of your top 3 resources or tools and introduce a soft pitch for your product.
Keep the language natural, conversational, and completely free of hype-based buzzwords.
